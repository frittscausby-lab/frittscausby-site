Fritts Causby

Fritts Causby is a freelance writer and ghostwriter whose work spans luxury real estate, architecture, travel, ecology, and local culture. The real estate pitches Fritts has created for Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices California Properties have informed features in Robb Report. Additional work has appeared in Wrightsville Beach Magazine and Port City Daily. He also publishes The Pinnacle, a website and newsletter on luxury real estate, stocks, and the aspirational lifestyle.
